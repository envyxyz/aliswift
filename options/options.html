AliSwift

Send AliExpress products to Google Sheets.

Your sheets

AliSwift saves each product to the highlighted sheet. Every sheet has its own columns, pricing and title rules. Use the pencil to edit one.

Add a Google Sheet first, then set your pricing here.

Cost price

Cost price is what one item costs you: item price, plus shipping when AliExpress shows it, plus the extra cost below. Your sell price is calculated from this total.

Sell price

Formula: type how to get your sell price from costPrice, for example (costPrice + 2) * 1.5. Use + - * / and brackets. Margin %: adds a percentage on top, so 25 makes the sell price 125% of your cost.
