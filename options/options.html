Cost price

Cost price is what one item costs you: item price, plus shipping when AliExpress shows it, plus the extra cost below. Your sell price is calculated from this total.

Sell price

Formula: type how to get your sell price from costPrice, for example (costPrice + 2) * 1.5. Use + - * / and brackets. Margin %: adds a percentage on top, so 25 makes the sell price 125% of your cost.

eBay fees

Used only for the profit in the dashboard's Inspect view: sell price x percentage, plus the fixed fee. The starting figures are eBay US's for most categories. Check your own marketplace's fee table and change them here.

Product title

AliSwift turns the AliExpress product name into the keywords it saves. It removes the words you list, then keeps the first few.

AI keys

Used for writing listings, filling item specifics and mapping an imported sheet. Free keys are used first, top to bottom; a Paid key takes over the moment every free key is busy. Keys stay on this device. They aren't synced or exported, and each is sent only to its own provider.

    Get API keys

    Gemini (Google): reads photos, the default. Get a key at Google AI Studio. More keys from the same Google Cloud project share one limit and add nothing: use a different project, or better, a different Google account.

    Groq: very fast, text only, with a free plan; its limits are per account, not per key. Get a key at console.groq.com.

    OpenRouter: one key for many models, free and paid, text only. Get a key at openrouter.ai. A Free key lists the free models only, and only models that can answer in JSON are offered. Free models allow about 50 requests a day until you have bought $10 of credit; a busy free model makes AliSwift move on to your next key.

    Check the free limits on each provider's page. Using several accounts to get past a provider's limits may break its terms: check them first.

    Listing instructions

    These rules tell Gemini how to write your title, item specifics and description. Edit them to match your style.

    Edit sheet

    Open sheet
    Sets the currency for this sheet and which eBay site opens when you list. If it's not set, prices are saved as shown, with no currency check.

    Columns

    Type the column letter where each field goes. Each product is added on a new row, just below the last row that has keywords, and never above the first data row. Optional columns switch on SKUs, status, images and profit.

    Orders

    Order columns Advanced

    Create sheet

    Creates a new Google Sheet in your Drive with Dashboard, Inventory and Orders tabs, in the marketplace's currency. Supplier prices are converted at the live exchange rate. To use a sheet you already have, close this and choose Import from Google Sheets.
    The sheet numbers each product with this in front: SKU-01, SKU-02 and so on. Up to 8 letters, numbers or dashes.

    Import sheet

    Pick a sheet and AliSwift works out its tabs and columns. It only reads your sheet. If it has no SKU or Image column, AliSwift asks before adding one.
